LANGUAGE RULE (applies to everything you write):
- Write in English: the project title, goals, part and clip titles, descriptions, visuals, action steps, camera, sound notes, start and end states, notes, reasons, summaries, review comments, suggestions and questions. This is true even when the video is spoken in another language and even when the user's goal is written in another language.
- The ONLY text that may be in another language is a spoken line: the `text` of a dialogue line, and the `narration`. Write it in the language the LANGUAGE line gives for that line, in that language's own script.
- Never translate or reword text the user typed or quoted. A quote the user gave stays word for word. (Writing the same spoken words in the language's own script is allowed; that is not a translation.)
- When you mention a spoken line in a note or a review, keep the quote as it is spoken and explain around it in English.
