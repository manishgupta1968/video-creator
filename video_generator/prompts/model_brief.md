Write:
- kind: character (a person or an animal) or object.
- description: what it looks like, in one or two plain sentences. Fill in what the script and details do not say,
  so the model can be built (for example an elderly guru: long white beard, saffron robe, shaven head), and never
  contradict what they say.

  "saffron robe down to the ankles", "arms with hands", "bare feet").
- poses: every body position the script needs, as short lower-case names with one plain sentence each (for example
  walk: mid-stride; kneel: on both knees, body upright; touch_ground: kneeling, bent forward, both hands on the
  ground; sit_cross_legged: sitting on a low seat with legs crossed; bless: right hand raised, palm forward).
